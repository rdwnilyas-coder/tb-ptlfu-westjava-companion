At a glance

Headline quantities from the study cohort and the transportable model.

Predictor value details

The article reports predictor importance and refers the reader here for the value-level detail. For each predictor the table gives the number of patients, the observed pre-treatment loss rate, and the odds ratio adjusted for the other predictors with a 95 % confidence interval. This is the one part of the analysis that has no figure in the article, because the detail is the point.

Transferability by district and city

Leave-one-district-out results for the transportable model: trained on 26 areas, tested on the one held out. Colour on the map is the observed rate minus the predicted rate, so red means the provincial model under-estimates risk there; hatching marks weak discrimination.

Area risk after case mix is equalised

From the development model, which includes district of residence. Reference category is Kabupaten Bogor.

Model comparison and calibration

Five explainable algorithms. Calibration is reported alongside discrimination because the two diverge sharply under class weighting: weighting leaves the ranking untouched while moving predicted risk far from the observed rate.

Effect of local recalibration

Within each held-out area a correction was estimated on one half of that area's patients and applied to the other half, and vice versa, so no recalibrated prediction was evaluated on the data that produced its own correction. This analysis has no figure in the article.

Integer risk scorecard

Points are the multivariable logistic coefficients divided by a fixed scaling constant and rounded to the nearest integer. Every level is listed, including those worth zero points, so any patient can be scored from this table alone.

Spatial statistics

Global Moran index computed on the per-area calibration gap under three neighbourhood definitions, with significance from 9,999 permutations. This analysis has no figure in the article.

Feature selection

Three strategies compared under the same leave-one-district-out pipeline. The medians coincide, so feature selection is a choice of interpretive purpose rather than a lever for performance.

Neither burden nor data quantity explains model fit

Model misfit across areas is driven by recording completeness and a residual local effect, not by how much tuberculosis an area carries or how many records it contributes.
