At a glance

Headline quantities from the study cohort and the transportable model.

Predictor value details

The article reports predictor importance but leaves the value-level detail to this page. For each predictor, the table gives the number of patients, the observed pre-treatment loss rate, and the odds ratio adjusted for the other predictors, with a 95 % confidence interval.

Transferability by district and city

Leave-one-district-out results for the transportable model: trained on 26 areas, tested on the one held out. The calibration gap is the observed rate minus the predicted rate, so a positive value means the provincial model under-estimates risk in that area.

Adjusted odds ratios by area

From the development model, which includes district of residence. Reference category is Kabupaten Bogor. These estimate area-level risk after patient case mix has been equalised.

Model comparison and calibration

Five explainable algorithms, each fitted with and without balanced class weights where the option exists. The Brier skill score is measured against a constant prediction at the observed prevalence; a positive value beats that baseline.

Effect of local recalibration

Within each held-out area a correction was estimated on one half of that area's patients and applied to the other half, and vice versa, so no recalibrated prediction was evaluated on the data that produced its own correction.

Integer risk scorecard

Points are the multivariable logistic coefficients divided by a fixed scaling constant and rounded to the nearest integer. Every level is listed, including those worth zero points.

Spatial statistics

Global Moran index and local indicators computed on the per-area calibration gap under three neighbourhood definitions, with significance from 9,999 permutations.

Feature selection

Three strategies compared under the same leave-one-district-out pipeline, plus the check that mutual-information selection carried out within each training fold gives the same result as selection on the whole dataset.

Learning curve

ROC AUC on a fixed held-out test set against increasing training set size, for the linear model and for the best-performing ensemble.
